Who we are

FUFON – Future For The Needy

FUFON – Future For The Needy is a community-based humanitarian organization co-founded by Ndimongang Ernest Chia and team in Oku, Bui, Northwest Region of Cameroon, in 2016.

We are committed to assisting the most vulnerable and less privileged, including persons with disabilities, orphans, abandoned people, people experiencing depression, and children who have dropped out of school.

Our belief: everyone deserves dignity, support and the opportunity to build a better future.

What guides us

Our mission

To help vulnerable people access essential support, learning opportunities and practical tools for a more secure and independent future.

Care that creates opportunity

We aim to respond to immediate needs while helping individuals and families strengthen their prospects for the future.
