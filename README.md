FUFON - Oku Bui, Northwest Cameroon

We assist orphans, less privileged & depressed children in education, the physically impaired, the struggling youths especially girls.

   Who We Serve
- Orphans and vulnerable children
- Less privileged families
- Depressed and traumatized children
- Physically/mentally impaired,   especially children
- Struggling youths, especially girls

  What We Do - 
We help in Education,pay school Fee and provide basic Materials Support.
- Support physically impaired children to access education
- Psychosocial support for the depressed children
- Empower struggling youths, especially girls with small start-up businesses 

 Location
Oku, Bui Division, North West Region, Cameroon

 Verification
Field forms verified by:
1. Parent/Guardian Representative
2. FUFON Volunteers
3. School administration 

  Partner
Working towards partnership with voluntary donor and great organizations like UNICEF for inclusive education.
